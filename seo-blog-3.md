## Quick Facts
- NRIs & OCI cardholders can buy residential/commercial property — no RBI approval
- Agricultural land, plantations & farmhouses: not permitted
- Registration can be completed from abroad via an attested Power of Attorney
- Payments routed through NRE / NRO / FCNR accounts only

> Note: General information only. Confirm your specific tax and legal
> position with a qualified professional before purchasing.
